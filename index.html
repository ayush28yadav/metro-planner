      About this data

      Platforms and exit gates come from each station's page on delhimetrorail.com (read in November 2026). Aqua Line platforms follow NMRC's layout: Platform 1 towards Sector 51, Platform 2 towards Depot. NMRC doesn't publish where each Aqua Line gate leads, so Aqua Line stations show the nearest landmark NMRC lists. Travel times are fitted to station-to-station times from delhimetrorail.info, separately for each direction (the Aqua Line uses NMRC's own station timetable); a few of the newest stations use their line's average time per stop. Changing lines adds 4 to 8 minutes. First and last trains come from terminal timings: DMRC's last trains (mostly 11:00 pm, the same every day), first trains around 6:00 am (7:00 am on Sundays on the Pink, Magenta and Grey lines), the Aqua Line from 6:00 am (8:00 am on Sundays) to 11:25 pm from Sector 51 and 10:10 pm from Depot, and Rapid Metro from 6:05 am to 10:00 pm. Fares use DMRC's slabs from 25 August 2025, and Airport Express fares aren't included. Official sources: delhimetrorail.com (link) and nmrcnoida.com (link).
